## Usage

Use it for any recipe or cooking how-to. The title sits on the faded lower edge of the cover `image`, followed by time, servings and difficulty chips and tinted `tags`. Each ingredient can be ticked off: the count and the thin bar above the list follow along, and ticked lines are struck through. Once the card is 520px wide, ingredients and steps sit side by side. The checklist is uncontrolled (`defaultChecked`) or controlled (`checked` with `onCheckedChange`). The default variant is a plain lobe-ui card with body-size type, `Tag` chips and plain step numbers.

## A2UI

`variant="a2ui"` takes the layout of the [A2UI playground](https://a2ui.open.bot/components/RecipeCard) recipe card: a 14px card, hairline chips, a purple-tinted tag and step numbers, and compact 13px type. Colours stay the lobe-ui theme's in both variants: the tints come from the `purple` preset palette.

## APIs

<Api name="RecipeCard" />

`checked`, `defaultChecked` and `onCheckedChange` use ingredient indexes. The `recipeCard.*` texts come from `@lobehub/ui/i18n`; `texts` overrides them (`servings` keeps a `{count}` placeholder).

## Styles

`@lobehub/ui/style.css` already includes the styles of `RecipeCard`. To load only the components you use, import this file instead of `style.css`:

```tsx | pure
import '@lobehub/ui/css/RecipeCard.css';
```
