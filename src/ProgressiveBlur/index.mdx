## Default

Without children, `ProgressiveBlur` is an absolutely positioned, `pointer-events: none` overlay. Place it next to the scroller inside a positioned container, so it stays on the edge while the content scrolls underneath.

<Demo of={DemoIndex} layout="bare" />

## Wrapper

With children it renders a relative container, makes the content scrollable and lays the blur band over its edge.

<Demo of={DemoWrapper} />

## Position

`position` picks the edge. For `left` and `right`, `height` is the width of the band.

<Demo of={DemoPosition} />

## Mode

`auto` renders the blur layers and drops them under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce` or when `backdrop-filter` is unsupported, keeping a stronger gradient so text stays readable. `blur` always renders the layers; `gradient` never does, which is the cheap option for long lists or low-end devices.
