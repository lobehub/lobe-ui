## A2UI

`variant="a2ui"` is the [A2UI playground](https://a2ui.open.bot/components/Section) `Section`: a 15px semibold title with its context (`eyebrow`) after it on the same baseline, a muted description, and the children 12px apart. The context is a sentence-case label, never an uppercase kicker above the title.
