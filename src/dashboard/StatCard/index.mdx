## Migration

`prominent` and `wash` are deprecated and ignored; the card always uses the MetricStrip item scale. `delta` no longer draws a trend icon, and `hint` sits after the delta on the same line.

## Header controls stay aligned

The label row has a fixed height. A `Segmented` or `ActionIcon` in `action` sits centered on the label and never makes its card taller, so values, previous-period lines and charts line up across a row whether or not a card has a control. The control sits on the trailing edge of the label row, and `chart` renders a sparkline or area chart at full card width under the delta and hint. Use compact controls there: `Segmented size="small"` and `ActionIcon size="small"`. `Surface` headers follow the same rule for `extra`.
