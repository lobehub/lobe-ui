## Default

The list highlights the longest matching path. Fold a group to hide its items, or switch to the icon rail. A badge becomes a dot on the rail. Inside [ConsoleShell](/components/dashboard/console-shell) the rail follows the shell unless `collapsed` is set.

<Demo of={DemoIndex} layout="bare" />

## Tree

Use `items` for top-level links and nested `groups` for a second level. Icons are optional: a group with an `icon` becomes a single rail button that links to its `href` (or its first item), and items without an icon are left off the rail. With `defaultExpanded="active"` every group starts folded and the branch holding the current page opens on navigation, then scrolls into view.
