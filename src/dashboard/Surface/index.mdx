With a title, description or extra it gets a header, a padded body whose trailing table meets the card edges, an optional chart slot, and first-load / refresh states. ## Header alignment

The header is one 32px control row plus 8px above and below, with or without `extra`, so a row of cards lines up. Controls in `extra` sit centered on the title line; use compact ones (`Segmented size="small"`, `ActionIcon size="small"`). A `description` adds its own line under the title and leaves `extra` on the title line, so cards with a description share one taller height.
