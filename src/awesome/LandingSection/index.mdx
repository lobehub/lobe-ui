## Default

Wrap `BentoGrid`, `CodeShowcase`, `FeatureGrid` or your own content in a LandingSection so every band shares the same rhythm. The heading sits on the left with a tag above it, and `actions` or `extra` sit on the right. Set `align="center"` to stack everything centered.
