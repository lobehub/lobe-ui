## Default

Grainient fills its nearest positioned ancestor. It needs WebGL2; without it nothing is drawn, so keep a background color on the parent.

<Demo of={DemoIndex} layout="bare" />

## Custom colors and theme

`lightMode="auto"` washes the colors toward white in the light theme.
