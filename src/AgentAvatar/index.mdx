## Default

`seed` (or `name` when `seed` is omitted) is hashed into a shape and a color, so the same seed always draws the same avatar with no randomness. `name` is also the accessible label; without a name or `aria-label` the avatar is decorative.

## Shapes

Twelve silhouettes share one construction: a flat body, the big eyes with whites, no mouth and no backing plate. Only the shape and the color vary, so every shape stays recognizable at 24px. `shape` overrides the derived one.

<Demo of={DemoShapes} />

## Colors

`color` overrides the derived color. Palette keys resolve to theme color scale steps, with lighter steps in dark mode so `ink` and `gray` keep their contrast against dark backgrounds. Any other string is used as a CSS color as is. The eyes keep the same warm white and near black in every color and appearance.

<Demo of={DemoColors} />

## States

`state` is expressed only through the eyes and motion: `idle` wanders and blinks, `working` looks down and scans, `waiting` looks at you and sways, `blocked` widens the eyes and shows an error badge, `thinking` looks up with rising thought bubbles, and `done` closes the eyes into a smile. Motion is off by default; `animated` plays it, and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` always falls back to the still pose. Animations are offset per seed so a list of avatars does not blink in sync.

## APIs

<Api name="AgentAvatar" />

`AGENT_AVATAR_SHAPES`, `AGENT_AVATAR_COLORS` and `AGENT_AVATAR_STATES` list the accepted values, and `getAgentAvatarTraits(seed)` returns the derived `shape` and `color` without rendering.
