## Example

Every part is a gray close to the page, in the light and the dark theme, so the loader sits in an empty area like a watermark. Use [LobotBrandLoading](/components/brand/lobot-brand-loading) when the brand mark itself should show.

<Demo of={DemoIndex} />

## Sizes

`size` is the box. The mark rests in its lower part, and the jump and the bubbles stay inside it.

<Demo of={DemoSizes} />

## Motion

The still mark renders first, also on the server. The volume engine of [AgentAvatar](/components/agent-avatar) loads on demand and takes over from it, and the loader stays still when reduced motion is preferred.
